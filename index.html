Street Rod

Procedural V8 sound + longitudinal driving demo, v1

Controls

↑Throttle (hold; it ramps up)
↓Brake (hold; ramps to full pedal, can lock the wheels)
→ / ←GT350: shift up / down (clutch is automatic).
Cadillac: selector N → D → S → L (Hydra-Matic shifts itself)
S or IIgnition: start / stop engine
1 / 2Select car
VListening position (outside rear / in-cabin approx.)
Ukm/h ↔ mph
TReset trip meter
MMute

Car

The engine sound is synthesised live in your browser (no recordings). The in-cabin view is a filtered approximation, not a measured cabin response.
